# Day 1: Descriptive Statistics & Distributions

**Focus:** Understanding data summaries and their role in ML.

---

## Machine Learning & Data

- Models summarize patterns in data
- Descriptive statistics (center, spread, shape) are the foundation of EDA and model evaluation

---

## Key Concepts

### Center
- **Mean:** Average of all values
- **Median:** Middle value (50th percentile)
- **Mode:** Most frequent value

### Spread
- **Range:** Max - Min
- **Variance:** Average of squared deviations from mean
- **Standard Deviation:** Square root of variance (same units as data)
- **IQR:** Q3 - Q1 (middle 50% of data)

### Skewness
- Mean > Median → Right-skewed (outliers pull mean up)
- Mean < Median → Left-skewed (outliers pull mean down)

### Percentiles & Quartiles
- **Q1:** 25th percentile
- **Median:** 50th percentile (Q2)
- **Q3:** 75th percentile
- Split data into four equal quarters
